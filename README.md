# Customer RFM Segmentation - Online Retail II UCI

## Overview: 
This project analyses transactional data from a UK-based online retailer to segment customers by loyalty and spending behaviour using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.

## Business Question: Which customers are drifting, and how much revenue is at risk? 
- How is the current customer loyalty base affecting revenue? 
- How much on average does each customer segment spend? 
- How many customers are there in each segment? 
- Are customers in the "Lost" segment worth re-engaging? 

## Key Findings
- Revenue at Risk (At Risk + Lost segments combined) = £2,725,905
- Champions have the highest average spend at £9,758 - Loyal Customers second at £3,740
- Needs Attention is the largest segment by customer count (1,728), with Lost second (1,090)
- Lost customers average only £190 spend — recovery cost likely exceeds return

## Recommendation
The At Risk segment represents £2,519,019 in revenue from customers who have spent well in the past but haven't purchased recently. These are the customers most worth targeting with re-engagement offers or promotions. The Lost segment averages only £190 per customer, the cost of a campaign to recover them would likely outweigh what they'd bring back in.
